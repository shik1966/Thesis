### **Immediate Action Items:**
1. **Choose your presentation software:**
   - PowerPoint (recommended for compatibility)
   - Google Slides (good for cloud backup)
   - Canva or Beautiful.ai (if you want modern templates)

2. **Gather your thesis images:**
   - All .png files from your thesis folder
   - Focus on: accuracy plots, loss curves, visual results, metrics comparisons
   - Key images you'll need: `accuracy and loss.png`, `dice.png`, `metrics.png`, result comparison images

3. **Open the slide content file:**

## **Time-Saving Template Suggestion:**

**Use this simple, clean layout for all slides:**
- White background
- Dark blue title text (size 32-36)
- Black bullet points (size 24-28)
- Images sized to be clearly visible
- Slide numbers in bottom right
- University logo if available

## **If You Get Stuck:**

### **Technical Issues:**
- Can't embed images? Save everything as PDF first
- Software crashing? Switch to Google Slides (web-based)
- Images too large? Use online image compressor

### **Content Issues:**
- Can't find specific results? Use placeholder text "Results from thesis page X"
- Too much text? Convert paragraphs to 3-4 bullet points maximum
- Unclear explanations? Write them in your own simple words

### **Motivation Issues:**
- Remember: You've already done the hard work (the research)
- This presentation is just telling people about your excellent work
- 5 days is plenty of time - you're not behind!
