## **Time-Saving Tips**

### **Efficient Slide Creation:**
- Use simple, clean templates (don't spend time on fancy designs)
- Copy-paste your actual result images directly from thesis folder
- Use bullet points instead of full sentences on slides
- Focus on 1-2 key points per slide maximum

### **Efficient Practice:**
- Practice in front of a mirror first
- Record on your phone for quick review
- Practice just the opening and closing perfectly
- Focus on smooth transitions between major sections

### **Quick Technical Setup:**
- Test everything today (Day 1) - don't wait
- Have 3 backup copies ready immediately
- Identify backup laptop option now
- Know your adapter situation for projectors

## **Red Flags to Avoid**

🚫 **Don't over-prepare** - 5 days is plenty, don't burn out  
🚫 **Don't memorize everything** - know your key points well  
🚫 **Don't change major content** - work with what you have  
🚫 **Don't ignore technical prep** - equipment failure is preventable  
🚫 **Don't skip sleep** - especially the night before

## **Emergency Shortcuts (If Behind Schedule)**

### **If Day 1-2 are Lost:**
- Use Google Slides for faster creation
- Focus on key slides only (introduction, methodology, results, conclusion)
- Use simple black text on white background
- Practice with rough slides rather than perfect ones

### **If Running Out of Time:**
- Prioritize opening statement, key results, and closing
- Prepare 2-minute elevator pitch version
- Focus on top 3 anticipated questions only
- Ensure technical setup works above all else

**You've got this! 5 days is actually a great timeline. Stay focused, stay confident, and execute the plan systematically. Good luck! 🎓**
